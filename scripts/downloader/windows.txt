Flobi Pulse {version} for Windows
=================================

INSTALL

1. Double-click "Flobi-Pulse-Setup-{version}.exe".

2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   That warning shows because the app isn't signed with a paid certificate. It's the
   same app your team builds from github.com/Moh-jbr/flobi-pulse.

3. Choose where to install it and click Install.
   A "Flobi Pulse" shortcut appears on your Desktop and in the Start menu.

4. Open Flobi Pulse and choose the service-account key file (.json) your admin gave you.
   Flobi Pulse only reads: it can't change anything in the cluster.

UPDATES

When a new version comes out, an "Update available" button appears at the top of the
app. Click it: Flobi Pulse downloads the update, installs it in the same folder and
restarts. Your settings and keys stay. You won't need this zip again.

UNINSTALL

Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Flobi Pulse > Uninstall.
