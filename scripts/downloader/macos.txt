Flobi Pulse {version} for macOS
===============================

INSTALL

1. Double-click "Flobi-Pulse-{version}.dmg" and drag Flobi Pulse into the Applications
   folder. Keep it in Applications, so it can update itself.

2. Open Applications and double-click Flobi Pulse.
   The first time, macOS says it can't check the app, because it isn't signed with a paid
   Apple certificate. It's the same app your team builds from github.com/Moh-jbr/flobi-pulse.
   Click "Done", then:
     System Settings > Privacy & Security > scroll down > "Open Anyway" next to Flobi Pulse,
   and confirm with your password or Touch ID. You only do this once.

3. Want it on the Desktop or in the Dock?
   Desktop: in Applications, hold Option + Command and drag Flobi Pulse to the Desktop
            (this makes a shortcut).
   Dock:    while it's open, right-click its Dock icon > Options > Keep in Dock.

4. Choose the service-account key file (.json) your admin gave you.
   Flobi Pulse only reads: it can't change anything in the cluster.

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

UPDATES

When a new version comes out, an "Update available" button appears at the top of the
app. Click it: Flobi Pulse downloads the update, replaces itself and restarts. Your
settings and keys stay. You won't need this zip again.

UNINSTALL

Quit Flobi Pulse (menu bar icon > Quit), then drag it from Applications to the Trash.
